# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Students and early researchers facing dense academic writing (especially biomedical / PubMed-style papers). They open the tool mid-reading when a passage is opaque, paste or upload it, and need a trustworthy plain-English explanation with real citations—not a chat toy.

## Product Purpose

DumbItDown turns dense research text into plain-English explanations grounded in a fixed PubMed corpus, with citation contribution scores, term tooltips, and follow-up Q&A. Success is: understand the passage faster, see which sources drove the answer, and continue asking without losing context. No signup; technical demo / research tool, not multi-tenant SaaS.

## Brand Personality

Cinematic archive · scholarly warmth · glass-over-parchment. Voice is clear and confident, never hype-y. Emotion: stepping into a living research library that happens to have an AI inside.

## Anti-references

Generic purple AI SaaS landings, three equal feature cards with Lucide icons as the whole story, neon glow dashboards, Inter-on-slate “Linear clone” tool UIs, cream-and-terracotta lifestyle DTC, and chatbots that feel like toys rather than research instruments.

## Design Principles

1. **Trust before spectacle** — Citations, scores, and source honesty outrank decorative motion.
2. **Show the archive** — The product should feel like a library instrument: parchment, amber light, glass panels over warmth.
3. **One job per surface** — Landing sells the door into the archive; `/app` is for paste → explain → cite → follow up.
4. **Clarity over clever copy** — Plain language in the UI matches the product promise.
5. **Motion serves immersion, not delay** — Cinematic where first impression matters; crisp and interruptible in the tool.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion` on all scroll/entrance animation. Body and placeholder text must meet ≥4.5:1 contrast on void / maroon surfaces. Keyboard paths for primary flows (paste, simplify, citations, chat). No zoom-blocking viewports.
