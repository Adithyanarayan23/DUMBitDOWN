## Sample 1 — Inflammation / cytokines (warm script default)

```
Chronic low-grade inflammation is characterized by elevated circulating levels of pro-inflammatory cytokines such as interleukin-6 (IL-6), tumor necrosis factor-alpha (TNF-α), and C-reactive protein (CRP). These mediators interact with insulin signaling pathways and endothelial function, contributing to metabolic syndrome and cardiovascular risk. Understanding how cytokine cascades propagate systemic effects is essential for interpreting clinical biomarkers in obesity and type 2 diabetes research.
```

## Sample 2 — Cancer / immunotherapy

```
Immune checkpoint blockade targeting PD-1/PD-L1 pathways can restore T-cell mediated antitumor responses in several solid tumors. Response rates vary by tumor mutational burden, microsatellite instability, and the composition of the tumor microenvironment, including regulatory T cells and myeloid-derived suppressor cells. Predictive biomarkers remain an active area of translational oncology research.
```

## Sample 3 — Neuroscience / synaptic plasticity

```
Long-term potentiation (LTP) at hippocampal synapses depends on NMDA receptor activation, calcium influx, and subsequent AMPA receptor trafficking to the postsynaptic membrane. Brain-derived neurotrophic factor (BDNF) modulates these processes and has been implicated in learning, memory consolidation, and mood regulation. Disruptions in synaptic plasticity are studied extensively in models of depression and neurodegenerative disease.
```
